## What the project is

ContextGuard is a student research project on computer-vision software that detects
people and office objects (a laptop and a backpack) in recorded video of a staged
office. It does not identify people. It uses no face recognition and does not judge
anyone's intentions.

## What you will do

You will act out short, scripted scenes, such as entering the room, sitting down,
picking up a laptop and leaving. You will be filmed by one fixed camera. No audio
is used. The session takes about ____ minutes.

## How the recordings are used and protected

- Recordings are used **only** for developing and evaluating this FYP and for its
  academic presentation and report.
- They are stored privately by the project team and are **not** uploaded to public
  sites, including the project's public code repository.
- In project files you are referred to by a code (e.g. "V1"), not by name.
- Still images from the recordings may appear in the project presentation or report
  **only if you tick the box below**.
- Recordings will be deleted by ______________ (date, e.g. end of the FYP), or sooner
  on request.

## Your rights

Taking part is voluntary. You may stop at any time, and you may ask for your
recordings to be deleted at any time before the deletion date, without giving a reason.
